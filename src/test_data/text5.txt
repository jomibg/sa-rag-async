Deep within an ancient forest grew the Great Oak, whose leaves were made of crisp parchment. This was the Library of Leaves, where every story ever forgotten by humans was stored. A tiny mouse named Barnaby was the head librarian, carefully organizing the fallen foliage by emotion.

One day, a weary knight stumbled into the grove, weeping because he had forgotten the face and name of the lady he once swore to protect. "Please," he begged the mouse, "help me find my memory."

Barnaby scurried through the roots, searching the piles of gold and copper leaves. He brought the knight a dry, amber leaf. As the knight touched it, the rustling vein of the leaf whispered a name: Elowen.

The knight’s eyes cleared, his heart warming as the memory rushed back. He thanked the tiny librarian, left a silver coin as a bookmark, and rode away to find his lost love, leaving Barnaby to gently sweep the forgotten words back into the earth.
