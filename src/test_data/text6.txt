Brimstone was a dragon of immense size and terrible reputation, but he harbored a secret passion: he loved delicate, hand-painted porcelain teacups. While other dragons hoarded gold and sharp swords, Brimstone’s cave was lined with fragile saucers and elegant teapots.

His greatest challenge was his own clumsy, fiery nature. One excited sneeze could shatter a century of craftsmanship. To pour a cup of chamomile tea, he had to use his massive claws with the microscopic precision of a surgeon.

One afternoon, a brave princess arrived to slay him, only to find the beast delicately holding a tiny pink teacup with his pinky claw extended. She paused, lowered her sword, and smiled. "Do you have an extra cup?" she asked.

Brimstone beamed, carefully setting down a floral kettle. They spent the evening chatting about glaze techniques, proving that peace is easily found when you stop to share a cup of tea.
