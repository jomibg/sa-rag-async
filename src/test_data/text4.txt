In a village where the world was entirely gray, a young girl named Maya found a box of glowing, forgotten paints in her attic. The villagers had long ago traded their colors to a greedy wizard in exchange for absolute certainty; they never suffered storms, but they never saw a sunset either.

Curious, Maya dipped her brush into a pot of vibrant sapphire and stroked it across the dull morning sky. A brilliant streak of blue ripped through the gray, and the villagers gasped in terror, shielding their eyes.

"You will bring the unpredictable rain!" the elders warned, locking her away.

But from her tower window, Maya kept painting. She splashed fiery oranges, deep magentas, and rich emeralds across the horizon. With the colors came the wind, the wild thunderstorms, and eventually, the sweet smell of blooming flowers. The villagers soon realized that the beautiful chaos of a colorful world was worth every single drop of rain.
