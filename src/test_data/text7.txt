Twinkle was the clumsiest star in the night sky. While the other stars held their coordinates perfectly to guide sailors, Twinkle stumbled over a passing comet and tumbled out of the constellation of Orion. But instead of falling down to Earth like a shooting star, Twinkle fell up.

He tumbled deeper into the great, dark void of the unknown universe, past the velvet curtains of the nebulae. He landed in a place where no light had ever shone—a lonely, frozen planet that had never known warmth.

As Twinkle sat up, his gentle, radiant light illuminated the icy plains, turning them into a shimmering garden of diamond reflections. The frozen planet thawed just enough to smile.

Twinkle realized he hadn't messed up his celestial duties at all. "Some stars are meant to guide the living," he whispered, settling into his new home, "and some are meant to bring light to the places everyone else forgot."
